# RS-MediaPlayer privacy policy

RS-MediaPlayer collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

- **Your sources** (server addresses, usernames, passwords and playlist addresses) stay on your
  device. Passwords are kept in the Keychain; addresses in the app's database are encrypted.
- **What you watch, rate and favorite** stays on your device, to show Recently Watched, Favorites
  and For You. Nothing of it leaves the device.
- **Network traffic** goes only to the sources you add, to load their lists, guides, logos and
  streams, and to Apple for the subscription.
- **Subscriptions** are handled by Apple. The app learns only whether a subscription is active.

Deleting a source removes its login, favorites and history. Deleting the app removes its lists and
history; iOS can keep logins in the Keychain after that, so delete your sources first to remove them
too.

RS-MediaPlayer doesn't include, sell or recommend any channels, playlists or providers.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/RoySchultz/rs-mediaplayer.
